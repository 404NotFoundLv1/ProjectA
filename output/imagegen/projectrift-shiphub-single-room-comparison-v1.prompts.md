## Shared brief

- Create a professional Unreal Engine environment concept sheet for the only major player-navigable room in the ship.
- All non-combat ship gameplay happens in one continuous open volume for 1–4 third-person players.
- No accessible side rooms, branching corridors, or separate cabins.
- Integrate armory/crafting, warehouse/loadout, medical/purification, communications/intelligence, role/equipment, and ship-repair/reactor monitoring into open wall alcoves, counters, sealed equipment bays, or glass-fronted machinery.
- Keep the shielded jump core visible but non-enterable; violet light is restricted to the core.
- Use the exterior concept only for rugged human-engineered gunmetal/cool-gray PBR identity, exposed repaired structural spine, amber maintenance lighting, sparse cyan screens, and restrained wear.
- Early-to-mid crash restoration state: reinforced broken-spine structure, welded patches, subtly mismatched replacement panels, protected conduits.
- Grounded realistic PBR, medium detail density, physically plausible modular construction.
- One dominant wide interior perspective from the deployment entrance at third-person camera height, plus a small top-down plan inset of the same layout.
- No readable text, labels, logos, watermark, decorative UI, people, space windows, luxury/utopian/gothic styling, excessive neon/greebles, or current UE demo-map geometry.

## Option A — Ring Operations Hall

- Broad rounded-rectangular hall, approximately 24 m × 20 m × 7 m.
- Low central mission/ready operations island.
- A generous unobstructed 4 m-wide circulation loop.
- All other functions distributed around the perimeter walls.
- Shielded jump core behind armored glass on the far focal wall.
- Four players should remain mutually visible with short routes and no chokepoints.
- Avoid making the room resemble a circular menu screen; use structural height, wall alcoves, and lighting hierarchy.

## Option B — Axial Maintenance Deck

- Long broad axial hall, approximately 30 m × 14 m × 7 m, aligned with the broken spine.
- A clear 5 m-wide central lane from deployment entrance, through the mission/ready table, toward the far jump core.
- Functional stations sequence along both long walls.
- Two lateral bypass lanes around the mission table.
- Preserve visibility while expressing a linear repair journey from damaged entry-side systems to the restored core.
- Avoid narrow-corridor feeling, bottlenecks, and excessive travel distance.

## Option C — Sunken Core Theater

- Broad oval or rounded-octagonal hall, approximately 24 m × 22 m × 9 m.
- A shallow 1.2 m sunken central command floor containing the mission/ready island.
- Open perimeter ring reached by two broad stairs and two accessible ramps.
- Low railings and uninterrupted sightlines ensure both levels read as one room.
- Shielded jump core mounted high on the far focal wall.
- Create spectacle without isolated upper corridors, stair chokepoints, or tall central obstructions.
