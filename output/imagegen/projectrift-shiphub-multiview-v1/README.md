# ProjectRift Ship Hub Multiview v1

Visual authority: `projectrift-shiphub-option-a-ue5-realistic-v9.png`.

This package is a review-stage multiview environment concept set. It is intended to expose the approved single-room ship hub from complementary camera positions before dimensional white-model production. Perspective images are visual references, not dimensional orthographic drawings.

## Views

1. `01-front-entry-to-pods.png` — established entrance-side hero view looking north toward the five hibernation pods.
2. `02-reverse-pods-to-airlock.png` — reverse view looking south toward the main pressure airlock.
3. `03-left-preparation-oblique.png` — preparation/armory/fabrication bay foreground and cross-room view.
4. `04-right-medical-core-oblique.png` — medical bay and protected jump-core foreground and cross-room view.
5. `05-high-corner-overview.png` — high perspective overview exposing the complete functional adjacency.
6. `06-low-angle-ceiling-structure.png` — low camera documenting the pressure shell, structural ribs and overhead service ring.

## Cross-view invariants

- One continuous single-floor chamber, approximately 28–30 m wide, 22–24 m deep and 7 m high.
- Exactly five backward-reclined hibernation pods on the north/rear wall.
- Exactly one enlarged circular mission/navigation table.
- Exactly one floating machine-intelligence maintenance construct.
- Exactly four flush floor charging sockets.
- South/front: main pressure airlock.
- West: combined preparation/armory/fabrication bay and localized repair interface.
- East: medical/life-support, role/loadout and protected dormant jump core.
- Clean realistic PBR language, sparse fasteners, broad practical lights and one localized damaged repair zone.

## Production note

Image generation cannot guarantee geometric identity across views. Blender white-model and measured camera renders must become the geometry authority before final asset modeling. Conflicts between these images should be resolved according to the cross-view invariants above, not copied literally.
